Welcome

QUASY 2027, the fourth workshop in the QUASY series, is dedicated to quantitative approaches in syntactic research. It will be held in Prague, Czech Republic, from 30 August to 3 September 2027, as part of SyntaxFest 2027.

The workshop brings together researchers who explore syntax with quantitative, statistical and computational methods. We welcome work on any area of syntax that is grounded in empirical data, including corpus studies, treebank analyses and statistical modelling.

Topics include

  • Word order
  • Syntactic structures and their distributions
  • Treebank and corpus methods
  • Hypothesis testing on syntactic data
  • Syntax modelling and its applications
  • AI-driven approaches to syntactic analysis
