Call for Papers

QUASY 2027, the fourth Workshop on Quantitative Syntax, invites submissions on quantitative, statistical and computational approaches to syntax.

Important dates

  1. Paper submission deadline22 April 2027
  2. Notification of acceptance3 June 2027
  3. Camera-ready papersTBA
  4. Conference30 Aug – 3 Sep 2027

All deadlines are 23:59 AoE (Anywhere on Earth).

Scope

QUASY is dedicated to quantitative approaches in syntactic research. The workshop brings together researchers who explore syntax with quantitative, statistical and computational methods. We welcome work on any area of syntax that is grounded in empirical data, including corpus studies, treebank analyses and statistical modelling.

Topics

Topics of interest include, but are not limited to:

  • Word order
  • Syntactic structures and their distributions
  • Treebank and corpus methods
  • Hypothesis testing on syntactic data
  • Syntax modelling and its applications
  • AI-driven approaches to syntactic analysis

The full list of topics will be announced together with the joint SyntaxFest 2027 call.
